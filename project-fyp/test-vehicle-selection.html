Vehicle Selection Feature

Interactive cart with country-specific pricing for Central Asian taxi fares

Select Button

Each vehicle card now has a "Select Vehicle" button. Click it to add the vehicle to your cart with all fare details.

Smart Cart

A floating cart appears showing your selected vehicle with complete fare breakdown including base fare, distance cost, waiting time, and night charges.

Country-Specific

The cart displays prices in the correct currency for each country (₼, ₸, soʻm, с, ЅМ, m) with accurate conversion rates.

How to Test the Feature

📋 Testing Steps:

  1. Set a destination: Click one of the buttons below to set a destination for a specific country
  2. Navigate to fares page: Go to /listings/fares in your application
  3. Browse vehicles: Scroll through the 6 different vehicle types available
  4. Click "Select Vehicle": Choose any vehicle to add it to your cart
  5. View cart details: The cart will automatically open showing:
    • Vehicle name, type, and emoji icon
    • Country and currency information
    • Complete fare breakdown
    • Total calculated price
  6. Clear selection: Click "Clear Selection" to remove the vehicle from cart
  7. Toggle cart: Use the floating cart button (bottom-right) to show/hide the cart

Key Features Implemented

  • Select Button: Green button on each vehicle card to select that vehicle
  • Floating Cart: Fixed position cart (top-right on desktop, full-screen on mobile)
  • Cart Toggle Button: Floating button (bottom-right) to show/hide cart
  • Badge Counter: Shows "1" when a vehicle is selected
  • Fare Breakdown: Displays base fare, per km cost, distance cost, waiting time, night charge (if applicable), and total
  • Country Info: Shows selected country flag and currency
  • Clear Function: Red button to clear the selection and reset the cart
  • Notifications: Success/info messages appear when selecting or clearing vehicles
  • Responsive Design: Works perfectly on mobile, tablet, and desktop devices
  • Night Charge Indicator: Special yellow badge for night charges (10 PM - 6 AM)
