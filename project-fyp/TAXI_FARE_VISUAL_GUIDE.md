## 🎬 Animation Effects

1. **Page Load:**
   - Cards fade in from bottom
   - Stagger effect (one after another)

2. **Hover:**
   - Card lifts up (-10px)
   - Shadow increases
   - Border glows

3. **Calculate Button:**
   - Pulse effect on click
   - Result slides in from right

4. **Country Change:**
   - Fade out old cards
   - Update data
   - Fade in new cards
